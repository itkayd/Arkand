Complaints

Tell us if something’s wrong

We’d always rather know — it’s how we put things right and get better.

We hope you’ll always be happy with us, but if something isn’t right, please tell us. We welcome feedback and complaints, and we treat them as a genuine chance to improve.

How to raise it

The quickest way is to talk to us directly — call 020 8050 0095 or email team@arkandcare.co.uk. You can raise something informally for a quick word, or make a formal complaint if you’d prefer. Either way, we’ll listen properly.

What happens next

We’ll acknowledge your complaint promptly, look into it fairly and thoroughly, keep you informed, and give you a clear response. We’ll always tell you what we’ve found and what we’re doing about it.

If you’re still not happy

If we can’t put things right to your satisfaction, you can ask the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman to look at your complaint — an independent, free service that handles adult social care complaints, including for people who arrange and pay for their own care (lgo.org.uk). You can also share concerns about a care service with the Care Quality Commission.

Raising a complaint will never affect the quality or kindness of the support you receive from us.
