Accessibility

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our website comfortably.

Many of the people who use this site are older, or are helping someone who is, so making it easy to use matters to us a great deal.

What we aim for

We aim to meet the WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standard. That means we work to make the site readable and usable with: clear, good-sized text and strong colour contrast; full keyboard navigation; support for screen readers; and respect for your device settings, including reduced-motion (our animations turn off automatically if you’ve asked your device to reduce motion).

If something’s hard to use

If any part of this site is difficult for you, please tell us — call 020 8050 0095 or email team@arkandcare.co.uk — and we’ll help, and happily give you the same information another way.

Ongoing

Accessibility is never finished. We keep testing and improving, and we welcome any feedback that helps us do better.
