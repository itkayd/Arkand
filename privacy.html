Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

How we look after the information you share with us.

Last updated: July 2026.

Who we are

Arkand Care Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is the data controller for the personal information you provide through this website. We are registered in England & Wales, Company No. 17291178, registered office 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NA. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

What we collect

When you contact us — through our form, by email or by phone — we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, contact details, and anything you tell us about the support you or your relative may need.

Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry and, if you decide to go ahead, to arrange and provide care. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you and, where care begins, performing our agreement with you. We never sell your information, and we won’t use it for marketing without your clear consent.

Sensitive information

You may share details about a person’s health or care needs. This is “special category” data, and we treat it with particular care — using it only to help with your enquiry and the support we provide, and keeping it secure.

Who we share it with

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where we are legally required to, or where there is a serious risk to someone’s safety (see our Safeguarding page). We use Formspree to receive enquiries submitted through our contact form; they process that data on our behalf under a duty to protect it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we reasonably need them to help you. If care doesn’t go ahead, we delete them; if it does, we keep records for as long as required and no longer.

Your rights

You have the right to ask to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email team@arkandcare.co.uk.

Cookies

Please see our Cookie Notice for how this website uses cookies.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will always be on this page.
