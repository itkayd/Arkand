Join us

Care the way it should be — want to be part of it?

We’re building our team carefully, and we’d love to hear from kind, reliable people.

Arkand Care is growing, and we’re always glad to hear from kind, reliable people who believe care should be personal, patient and done properly. Every carer who joins us will be carefully chosen, fully DBS-checked, and supported to do their best work — because the people we look after deserve exactly that.

If that sounds like you, we’d love to hear from you.

How to apply

Get in touch, simply

There’s no portal and no long form. Email a few lines about yourself and any experience you have to team@arkandcare.co.uk, or call and ask to talk about working with us. You’ll speak to the person you’d actually be working with.
