Join us

Care the way it should be done

If you work in care the way we mean to — the same face, unhurried visits, honesty with families — we'd like to hear from you.

What we look for

More about how you are than what's on paper

Arkand is founder-led, and it will grow slowly and carefully. The people we work with need to fit the promises we make to families.

You keep your word

You turn up when you said you would, and if something changes you say so early. Families plan their week around us.

You're unhurried

You understand that thirty minutes is a minimum, and that the conversation is often the care.

You notice things

You spot when something's a little off and you're comfortable saying so, kindly and early.

You're honest about scope

You do what's agreed and no more, and you never pretend a job is within reach when it isn't.

You're local, or want to be

You know Chelmsford and the villages, or you're keen to — because short journeys mean more time with people.

You have the right to work in the UK

We'll talk through the practical checks together. Nothing to prepare before you get in touch.

How to apply

Get in touch, simply

There's no portal and no long form. Email a few lines about yourself and any experience you have to team@arkandcare.co.uk, or call and ask to talk about working with us. You'll speak to the person you'd actually be working with.

We read everything that comes in, and we'll reply.
