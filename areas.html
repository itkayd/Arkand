Areas we cover

Home care across East London

We keep our area deliberately local — East London. A carer who spends an hour in traffic is a carer who isn't with you.

Where we work

Arkand Care provides companionship and help at home right across East London. Keeping the area close to home is a deliberate choice: it means a familiar carer can arrive unhurried and on time, and be there again next week — the same face, not a different name each visit.

Because we stay local, we can be flexible about the little things — a shorter notice, an extra visit in a difficult week, or a change of time — without a long drive getting in the way.

A familiar, local face

The same trusted carer, close to home — someone you get to know, not a stranger sent from far away.

On time, unhurried

Short journeys mean visits that start when they should and never feel rushed.

Flexible when life changes

Staying local lets us adjust visits around your week — quietly, and without fuss.

Not sure if we reach you?

Ring and ask. If you're just outside the area we'll say so plainly, rather than promise a carer who arrives late and rushed.
