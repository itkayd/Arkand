Areas we cover

Chelmsford and the villages around it

We keep our area deliberately tight — Chelmsford and ten neighbouring villages. A carer who spends an hour in traffic is a carer who isn't with you.

We cover Chelmsford and the villages within a short drive of it. Choose a place to see how we help there — or just call, and we'll tell you honestly whether you're inside our patch.

Where we work

Chelmsford

Essex's county town and our anchor. From the cathedral quarter and the shops around High Chelmer and Bond Street to the quieter streets off Springfield Road and Broomfield Road, most of our visits are a short drive apart. That closeness is deliberate: it means a carer can be with you when they said they would, and can call in again if the day doesn't go to plan.

Often asked for here: Companionship, a walk along the Can in Central Park, or a lift into town for the shopping.

Where we work

Great Baddow

One of the largest villages in the country, spread from The Vineyards and Baddow Road down towards Meadgate. It's big enough that neighbours don't always know each other, which is exactly why a familiar weekly face matters here. We know the parades of shops and the lanes well enough to make outings easy.

Often asked for here: A regular visit near St Mary's, or accompanied trips to the local shops.

Where we work

Springfield

From Chelmer Village and Springfield Green across to the older streets near St Augustine's, Springfield runs right up to the river. It's a settled, green part of Chelmsford where a lot of people have lived for decades — and where staying in a house full of memories is often the whole point of getting a little help.

Often asked for here: Help at home, the laundry and ironing, and company through a quiet afternoon.

Where we work

Broomfield

North of the city and best known for Broomfield Hospital, so this is a village where families are often thinking about an elderly relative getting home and settled after a stay. We don't do the medical side, but we can be the friendly face doing the shopping, the tidying and the regular check-ins around it.

Often asked for here: Companionship and help at home along Broomfield Road and the closes off Patching Hall Lane.

Where we work

Writtle

The village green, the duck pond and the pubs around it make Writtle one of the prettiest spots near Chelmsford — and the birthplace of Britain's first regular radio broadcasts, back in 1922. It's a place people are proud to live, and an easy, pleasant one for an accompanied walk or a coffee out.

Often asked for here: Accompanied outings to The Green, or a steady hand for errands along the Lordship Road.

Where we work

Danbury

Up on one of the highest ridges in Essex, with Danbury Common and the country park on the doorstep. The hills that make it beautiful also make everyday jobs harder as people get older, so a lift, a bit of shopping or company on a short walk goes a long way here.

Often asked for here: Shopping, prescriptions from the village, and company with a view over the common.

Where we work

Galleywood

Built around its common and the old racecourse — famously the only course in the country to run right around a church. It's a village of green space and winding roads south of the city, where we help people stay independent without the isolation that quiet lanes can bring.

Often asked for here: Companionship, light help at home, and outings across Galleywood Common.

Where we work

Moulsham

One of the oldest parts of Chelmsford, from Moulsham Street's independent shops and cafés to the streets around the mill. It's central and walkable, which makes accompanied outings genuinely enjoyable — a proper morning out rather than a chore.

Often asked for here: A walk to Moulsham Street for coffee, the shopping, or simple meals back at home.

Where we work

Boreham

A village with a long history — from New Hall to Boreham House — set just off the A12 to the north-east. Being a little out of the city can mean fewer casual visitors through the week, so a reliable, regular call from someone who knows you matters all the more.

Often asked for here: Regular companionship visits and help with shopping and prescriptions.

Where we work

Little Waltham

A Chelmer-side village north of the city, strung along The Street and the lanes towards Blasford Hill. It's small and quiet, the kind of place where an unhurried thirty minutes — or a good deal longer — makes the whole day feel less empty.

Often asked for here: Company through the afternoon, the ironing done, and a lift when it's needed.

Where we work

Stock

South towards Billericay, Stock is a picture-postcard village with its windmill, church and welcoming pubs around the green. The distance from the shops is part of its charm and part of the challenge — which is where accompanied outings and a hand with the shopping come in.

Often asked for here: Accompanied trips from the village, shopping, and simple meal preparation at home.
