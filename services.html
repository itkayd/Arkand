What we do today

Unregulated services, provided today, with nothing on this page that we can't actually deliver.

Companionship

Someone to sit with over a pot of tea, share the news of the day, play cards, or simply not be alone in the house. For many families, this is where it starts.

Help at home

Light cleaning, tidying, the laundry and the ironing. Not a deep clean — the everyday upkeep that keeps a home comfortable and safe to move around in.

Shopping & prescriptions

The weekly shop done together or on your behalf, a few bits picked up between visits, and prescriptions collected from the pharmacy.

Simple meal preparation

A hot meal made with the toaster, microwave and air fryer. No oven, no hob, no flame — we tell you that plainly, because the honesty is the point.

Accompanied outings

A steady arm to an appointment, the shops, church, the hairdresser, or a walk in the fresh air. Getting out is often the thing that lifts the week.

Respite sitting

Companionship-based sitting so a family carer can keep their own appointment, rest, or leave the house — knowing someone familiar is there.

Common questions

Questions families ask

Do you provide personal care, like washing and dressing?

Not yet. Washing, dressing and medication are regulated activities, and we will not offer them until the Care Quality Commission has registered us. Today we provide companionship, help around the home, shopping, simple meal preparation and accompanied outings — none of which are regulated.

What does “simple meal preparation” actually mean?

Meals made with the toaster, microwave and air fryer. No oven, no hob, no flame. We state it plainly because it's the honest scope of what we can do today.

Is there a minimum visit length?

Yes — thirty minutes. That's our minimum, not our default. Most visits are longer, because unhurried is the point.

How much do you charge?

We agree one clear hourly rate in writing before any care starts, with no travel charge, no assessment or set-up fee, and no minimum contract. We confirm your rate at the free home visit, so we can talk it through in person rather than quote a number that doesn't fit your situation.

Do I have to sign a contract on the day?

No. The home visit is free and there's nothing to sign while we're there. You decide in your own time.

Which areas do you cover?

East London. We keep the area tight on purpose.
