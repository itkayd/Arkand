What we do today

Everyone’s different, so nothing here is fixed. We’ll shape our help around what makes a difference for you — and change it whenever you need.

Companionship

A friendly face and good conversation. Someone to share a cup of tea, a walk, a hobby or a memory with — because company matters just as much as practical help.

Help at home

Keeping things comfortable and safe: light housework, laundry, tidying, washing up, changing the bed, and keeping walkways clear. All the lifting and carrying is done by us.

Meal preparation

Preparing fresh meals and snacks, batch-cooking to leave ready portions, and keeping drinks within easy reach — so eating well is never a chore.

Shopping & errands

Shopping with you or for you, collecting prescriptions, posting letters, and handling the little tasks that add up. You choose; we carry.

Getting out & about

A hand to get to appointments, the shops, a club or a friend’s — at your pace, with time to rest, so the world stays open to you.

Respite for family carers

Looking after someone you love is a lot. We step in so family carers can rest, work, or simply have a break — knowing their person is in kind hands.

No surprises

How it works, and what it costs

Honest about scope

What we can’t do yet

Arkand Care Ltd is registered at Companies House in England and Wales (no. 17291178). We are not registered with the Care Quality Commission, and we do not provide personal care.

Companionship, help around the home, shopping, meal preparation and getting out and about are not regulated activities, and we provide them today. Washing, dressing and medication are regulated, and we won’t offer them until we’re registered to.

We would rather tell you what we cannot do than be vague about what we can.

Coming soon — subject to CQC registration

Personal care — washing, dressing and helping with medication — is a regulated activity. We’re completing our CQC registration so we can offer it in future, and we won’t until we’re registered to.

In the meantime, if personal care is what’s needed now, we’re glad to point you towards a registered provider — just ask.

  • Personal care (washing, dressing)
  • Help with medication
  • Dementia support
  • Night & overnight care

Common questions

Questions families ask

Do you provide personal care, like washing and dressing?

Not yet. Personal care is a regulated activity, and we won’t offer it until we’re registered with the Care Quality Commission. Today we provide companionship and help at home, which don’t require CQC registration. If personal care is what’s needed now, we’re glad to point you to a registered provider.

Is there a minimum visit?

We’re flexible. Visits usually start at an hour, but if all that’s needed is a half-hour drop-in — a quick check, a bit of company, a hand with something — that’s absolutely fine too.

How much does it cost?

We don’t publish prices, because everyone’s needs are different. We talk it all through at the free home visit and agree everything in writing before any care begins — with no long contracts and nothing to sign on the day.

Will it be the same person each time?

Wherever we possibly can, yes — consistency is the whole point. A familiar face means trust can grow, and you never have to explain yourself twice.

Do I have to commit to anything?

No. The first visit is free, with no obligation, and there are no long contracts. We’d rather earn your trust every week.
