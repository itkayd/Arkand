Honest about scope

Where we stand with the CQC

Arkand Care Ltd is not currently registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). We provide companionship and practical home help only. We do not provide personal care, including help with washing, dressing or toileting. We do not prompt, administer or manage medication. We can collect a sealed prescription and hand it to you. Personal care is planned only after successful CQC registration.

We would rather tell you what we cannot do than be vague about what we can.

Common questions

Questions families ask

Is this the same as home care?

People often use 'home care' broadly. Arkand currently offers companionship and practical home help only, not regulated personal care.

Do you provide personal care, like washing and dressing?

Not yet. We do not provide personal care, including help with washing, dressing or toileting. Today we provide companionship and practical home help only. Personal care is planned only after successful CQC registration.

What does “simple meals” actually mean?

Straightforward meals and drinks prepared using the appliances agreed in the support plan. We agree what we will and won't use, in writing, before the service starts.

Is there a minimum visit length?

Yes — thirty minutes. That's our minimum, not our default. Most visits are longer, because unhurried is the point.

How much do you charge?

We agree one clear hourly rate in writing before the service starts, with no travel charge, no assessment or set-up fee, and no minimum contract. We confirm your rate at the free home visit, so we can talk it through in person rather than quote a number that doesn't fit your situation.

Do I have to sign a contract on the day?

No. The home visit is free and there's nothing to sign while we're there. You decide in your own time.

Which areas do you cover?

East London. We keep the area local on purpose so visits are dependable and unhurried.
