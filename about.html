About Arkand

Care that starts with the person, not the task.

Founder-led home care, delivered with dignity.

Arkand Care was founded on a simple belief: that everyone deserves to grow older with dignity, in the comfort of their own home, supported by people who genuinely care. We’re a local, independent company — small on purpose. That means you get to know us, and we get to know you.

Our name sits above everything we do, and our promise is in our words: delivered with dignity. It shapes how we turn up, how we speak, and how we treat the people we support and their families — the way we’d want our own loved ones treated.

We’re led personally by our director, who is completing a Level 5 Diploma in Leadership & Management for Adult Care and building Arkand toward full CQC registration. Right now we offer companionship and help at home — the kind of practical, everyday support that makes life easier and safer — and we’re always honest about exactly what we can and can’t do.

Honest about scope

Where we stand with the CQC

Arkand Care Ltd is registered at Companies House in England and Wales (no. 17291178). We are not registered with the Care Quality Commission, and we do not provide personal care.

Companionship, help around the home, shopping, meal preparation and getting out and about are not regulated activities, and we provide them today. Washing, dressing and medication are regulated, and we won’t offer them until we’re registered to.

We would rather tell you what we cannot do than be vague about what we can.
