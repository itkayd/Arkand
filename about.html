Our story

Care that began with a simple belief

That growing older should never mean growing apart from the comforts of home, or the warmth of good company.

Why we’re here

We started Arkand Care to bring a little more warmth to everyday life.

So many older people simply want to stay in the home they love — surrounded by their own things, their own routines, and their own memories — with a friendly hand when they need one.

Arkand Care exists to make that possible. We’re a small, caring team who believe home care should feel personal and human: the same familiar faces, genuine kindness, and time taken to do things properly. Not a call centre. Not a rush. Just good people you can trust, treating your home and your loved ones with the respect they deserve.

The name Arkand — and our geometric “A”, rising to a golden peak — speaks to the idea of the threshold: the care and respect of being welcomed into someone’s home. It’s a privilege we never take lightly.

Our promise

Not a call centre. Not a rush.

Just good people you can trust — with the time to do things properly.

We keep our visits unhurried on purpose, because the best care happens over a cup of tea and a real conversation. That’s the kind of company we’d want for our own families, and it’s the only kind we’ll offer yours.

What we stand for

The values behind every visit

Warmth first

We lead with kindness. Every visit should feel like a friend calling round, not a service being delivered.

Trust & honesty

We say what we mean and only promise what we can deliver — including being honest about what we can’t offer yet.

Dignity & respect

Unhurried, gentle care that puts each person’s comfort, choices and independence first — always.

Reliability

We turn up when we say we will, with the same familiar faces — because dependability is its own kind of care.

An honest word about our registration

Where we are with CQC

We believe in being completely open, so here’s exactly where we stand.

We’re fully insured, and a registered company with policies built to care-sector standards. We’re currently completing our registration with the Care Quality Commission (CQC) so that we can offer personal care in the future.

Right now, we provide companionship and help at home — services which don’t require CQC registration. We would never claim that status until the process is fully complete, and we’ll never offer regulated personal care before we’re able to do so properly. That honesty is part of the care.
