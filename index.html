Good to know

Questions families often ask

Can’t see what you’re wondering about? We’d be glad to talk it through — just get in touch.

Will it be the same person each visit?

Wherever we possibly can, yes. We believe familiar faces are at the heart of good care, so we work hard to send the same kind, trusted companion each time — someone you’ll get to know and look forward to seeing.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

Not at all. There are no long contracts and no tie-ins. You can start small, change things, or pause whenever you need to. Care should fit around your life, never the other way round.

Do you cover my area?

The best thing to do is get in touch. Tell us a little about where you are and what you need, and we’ll happily let you know how we can help.

Are you registered with the CQC?

We’re a registered company with policies and practices built to care-sector standards. We’re currently completing our registration with the Care Quality Commission so we can offer personal care in the future. Right now we provide companionship and help at home, which don’t require CQC registration — and we’ll always be honest about that.

Can you help with personal care, like washing or dressing?

Not just yet. Personal care is a regulated service, so we’ll only offer it once our CQC registration is complete. In the meantime we’re glad to note your interest, and to help with companionship and practical support around the home.

How do we get started?

Simply call us on 020 8050 0095 or send a message. We’ll have a warm, no-obligation chat, arrange a friendly visit to understand what would help, and take it gently from there.
