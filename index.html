Companionship & help at home

A friendly helping hand, right where the heart is — at home.

Delivered with dignity.

We help older people live comfortably and independently in their own homes — with good company, a hand around the house, and someone reliable who genuinely turns up.

Arrange a free visit See how we help

Growing older shouldn’t mean giving up the home you love, or the life you’re used to. Sometimes it just takes a little help — someone to talk to, a hand with the shopping, a warm meal, a lift to an appointment. That’s what we’re here for. Not clinical, not rushed. Just kind, steady support from someone who becomes a familiar face.

Why families choose us

Kind, honest, and genuinely reliable

The same familiar face

Not a different stranger every week. We send someone you know and trust, so you never have to explain yourself twice.

Honest, always

We’ll only ever offer what we can genuinely do well, and we’ll always be straight about what we can’t. No overpromising.

Led personally

When you ring, you speak to a real person — our director — not a call centre reading from a script.

Founder-led

The person responsible for your care is usually the person who answers the phone.

How we help

Everyday support that makes a real difference

Practical, everyday help that keeps life comfortable and safe at home.

Companionship

A friendly face and good conversation — someone to share a cup of tea, a walk or a memory with.

Help at home

Light housework, laundry, tidying and keeping things comfortable and safe. We do the lifting and carrying.

Meal preparation

Fresh meals and snacks prepared, with drinks kept within easy reach — so eating well is never a chore.

Shopping & errands

Shopping with you or for you, prescriptions collected, and the little tasks that add up handled.

Getting out & about

A hand to appointments, the shops or a friend’s — at your pace, so the world stays open to you.

Respite for family carers

We step in so family carers can rest, work or take a break — knowing their person is in kind hands.

More ways to help, once we’re registered

Arkand Care Ltd is registered at Companies House in England and Wales (no. 17291178). We are not registered with the Care Quality Commission, and we do not provide personal care.

Companionship and help at home don’t require CQC registration, and we provide them today. We’re completing our CQC registration so we can offer personal care in future — and we won’t until we’re registered to.

We would rather tell you what we cannot do than be vague about what we can.
